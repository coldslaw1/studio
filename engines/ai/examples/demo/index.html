Walk arrows or WASD, sneak Shift, hit Space, talk E, fast time T, show what they see and plan O, pause P.

Villagers keep a daily plan (home, work, inn, bed), guards patrol and answer calls for help, wolves hunt as a flanking pack, goblins surround and lose their nerve, the boar charges, the shaman keeps its distance, slimes never give up. Agents far from you fade: they are simulated in less detail. Hit a villager in front of the others and the village turns on you.
