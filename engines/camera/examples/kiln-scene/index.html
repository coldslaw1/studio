Mouse wheel (or + and -): zoom. Q and E, or drag on the picture: turn the camera. 0: back to the first view. Arrow keys or WASD: walk. Shift: run. J: swing. G: wave. K: fall down / get up. The characters are rigs animated live by the puppet engine: they turn to face where they go, blend from walk to run, and the guard watches you when you are near. Go behind a house or a tree and you are hidden by it. O: switch depth sorting off and on. Water, buildings, trees, fences and the guard stop you, and you slide along them. Characters are drawn life size; add ?scale=2 to the address for bigger ones, ?detail=6 for finer ones, and ?outlines to see the collision shapes. The camera is the camera engine (vendor/camera): it follows you, eases every move, and stays sharp when zooming by switching to finer pictures of the hamlet, which is baked from 24 angles.
